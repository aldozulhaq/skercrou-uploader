Skercrou Uploader

Skercrou Uploader is a private command-line tool that uploads videos to the YouTube channel Skercrou, which publishes highlight videos and Shorts of the owner's own Dota 2 matches.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the channel owner has already edited and approved by hand, with the title, description, tags and thumbnail the owner wrote.
  • Schedules each upload for a publishing time the owner chose.
  • Reads the owner's own channel and videos to confirm that an upload finished processing.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner, on their own computer, signed in with their own Google account. The tool has no other users, no public sign-up and no hosted service.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
